The APU Valorant Club is a student-led community focused on esports, teamwork, and growth.

Competition

We host tournaments and scrims to sharpen competitive skills.

Fun

Our History

🏛️ National Esports Elite Status

Beyond local tournament results, APU has been officially selected by Malaysia’s Ministry of Higher Education as the country’s first eSports Focal Sports Centre Coordinator. The university robustly supports players with expert coaching, professional practice facilities, competitive equipment, and international competition travel, cementing it as Malaysia's absolute benchmark for collegiate esports.

🕒 Historical Esports Journey

📅 2024 — InterVarsity Supremacy

🏆 Hosted & Won the First APU InterVarsity VALORANT Tournament

Organized entirely by APU students and supported by Malaysia’s Ministry of Higher Education. 18 universities and 24 elite teams competed across a RM5,000 prize pool.

🥇 1st: APU | 🥈 2nd: Sunway University | 🥉 3rd: UiTM

📅 2023 — Regional Grand Finals

🥈 Campus Legends MY/SG/PH Regional – 2nd Place

Represented Malaysia on the international stage against dominant university rosters from Singapore and the Philippines, finishing as the regional runner-up.

🥇 Campus Legends Malaysia 2023 Champion

Went completely undefeated throughout the national tournament, knocking out TARUMT, MMU, Taylor's University, and Sunway University to become the inaugural national champion.

📅 2022 — The First Major Title

🥇 Champion – UNITEN VALORANT Championship 2022

Secured one of APU’s first major VALORANT championship titles, proving our roster can outplay Malaysia’s toughest collegiate teams.

📅 2021 — Program Expansion

🥈 2nd Place – Predator Championship 2021

Continued steady structural growth within competitive esports programs across national circuits.

📅 2020 — Foundational Roots

🥉 3rd Place – Campus Esports Showdown

One of APU's earliest breakthrough milestones (achieved in Mobile Legends). This win served as the catalyst to officially establish the club infrastructure.
