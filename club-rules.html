Respect All Members

Treat every member with respect regardless of their skill level. Toxic behaviour, harassment, discrimination, or offensive language will not be tolerated.

Teamwork & Communication

Members should communicate politely during matches and practice sessions. Cooperation and teamwork are essential to achieving success.

Fair Play

Cheating, exploiting bugs, using third-party software, or engaging in any unfair gameplay is strictly prohibited.

Attend Activities

Members are encouraged to participate in club events, training sessions, and tournaments regularly to support the club community.
