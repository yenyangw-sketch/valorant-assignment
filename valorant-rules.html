Home / Rules / Valorant Rules

No Cheating

Using cheats, hacks, scripts or any third-party software is strictly prohibited. Members must always play fairly.

Respect Your Teammates

Players should communicate politely and avoid toxic behaviour. Respect every teammate and opponent.

Follow Official Valorant Rules

All club matches follow the official Valorant game rules and tournament regulations set by Riot Games.

Good Sportsmanship

Win or lose with respect. Encourage teammates and maintain a positive attitude during every match.
